Visa & Immigration Services

Tailored visa solutions for employment, study, marriage, long-term residency (F-2/F-5), and naturalization in South Korea.

🛂

Ministry of Justice Certified Immigration Agency

Immigration policies in Korea change frequently and strictly require legal justification. As an officially registered agency, we represent foreign nationals and corporate employers directly before Korean Immigration Offices to secure high approval rates.

Personal & Employment Visas

Comprehensive legal support for all foreign residence statuses.

E-7

E-7 Professional Visa

For foreign professionals hired by Korean companies (IT engineers, marketers, executives). We draft comprehensive 'Statement of Employment Reasons' to overcome strict corporate hiring quotas.

D-2

Student & Job Seekers (D-2 / D-10)

Visa extension for international students, changing status to D-10 (Job Seeker Visa), and smooth transition from student status directly into E-7 professional work visas upon graduation.

F-6

F-6 Marriage Immigration

Marriage visa for spouses of Korean citizens. We meticulous assist in proving genuine relationship history, income requirements, and housing proof to prevent visa rejections.

F-5

F-2 Points & F-5 Permanent Residency

F-2-7 points-based resident visa and F-5 Permanent Residency acquisition. We evaluate GNI income proofs, KIIP Korean language program completion, and criminal background verifications.
