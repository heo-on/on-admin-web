Understanding Authentication Types

We provide all forms of document authentication in a one-stop service.

Authentication Type Issued By Purpose & Usage
1. Certificate of Translation Licensed Admin. Attorney Certifies that the translation accurately reflects the original document. Accepted by most Korean government agencies (Immigration, Police, etc.).
2. Notary Public (Translation) Notary Lawyer Office A lawyer officially notarizes the signature of the translator. Required by banks, courts, or specific overseas institutions.
3. Apostille Ministry of Justice / MOFA An international certification making the document legally valid in all 131+ Apostille Convention member countries (e.g., USA, UK, Japan).
4. Embassy Legalization Foreign Embassies in Korea Required for Non-Apostille countries (e.g., China, Vietnam, UAE). Requires MOFA Consular Confirmation before Embassy Authentication.
