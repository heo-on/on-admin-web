Corporate & Investment Visas

We find the exact visa type for your business structure.

D-7 Visa

Intra-Company Transferee

For essential professionals or executives dispatched from an overseas headquarters to a foreign corporate Branch, Liaison Office, or Subsidiary in Korea.

  • ✔ Must have worked at HQ for at least 1 year.
  • ✔ Applies to Branch & Liaison setups.
Most Popular

D-8 Visa

Corporate Investment

For foreign investors or indispensable professionals dispatched to a Foreign-Invested Company (FDI) legally established in Korea.

  • ✔ Minimum FDI investment: 100 Million KRW.
  • ✔ Must hold at least 10% of total voting shares.

D-9 Visa

International Trade / Business

For individuals establishing a private company (sole proprietorship) in Korea, or those engaged in specialized international trade, export, or ship-building supervision.

  • ✔ Private business investment: 300 Million KRW.
  • ✔ Or based on high international trade volume.

FDI One-Stop Procedure

1

FDI Reporting & Document Prep

We report the Foreign Direct Investment to a designated foreign exchange bank and prepare all required incorporation documents (Apostilled POA, Passport copies, etc.).

2

Capital Remittance

The foreign investor remits the investment capital (e.g., 100M KRW) to a temporary bank account in Korea under the prospective company's name.

3

Corporate & Tax Registration

We handle the court registry for corporation establishment and register the business license with the local tax office.

4

Bank Account & FDI Company Registration

Open the official corporate bank account, transfer the capital, and officially register the entity as a Foreign-Invested Company.

5

Corporate Visa Issuance (D-7/D-8/D-9)

Finally, we apply for the appropriate corporate visa and Alien Registration Card (ARC) for the executives and dispatched employees.
