Statutory Certified Translation & Administrative Legal Office

Under Article 20 of the Administrative Scrivener Act of Korea, only licensed Certified Foreign Language Administrative Attorneys are legally authorized to issue official "Certificates of Translation Confirmation" for submission to government bodies and judicial authorities. As a fully licensed administrative law office, we provide translation and legalization services strictly compliant with official standards for global immigration agencies (such as IRCC, USCIS), embassies, and international corporate transactions.

📌 Statutory Obligations & Scope of Liability

A Certificate of Translation Confirmation is an official statutory procedure verifying that the translation faithfully and accurately reflects the original text. Its legal scope of liability is defined as follows:

  • • This document certifies that the translation was executed faithfully upon the client's request and accurately reflects the original document provided.
  • • The translator and administrative attorney do not perform substantive factual adjudication regarding the authentic provenance, forgery, or alteration of the underlying original document.

In summary, the certified administrative attorney does not adjudicate underlying private disputes, but assumes full statutory legal liability for the precise semantic conformity, completeness, and fidelity between the translated text and the submitted original document.

Certification Types Compared: Certificate of Translation vs Notarized Translation vs Apostille

Each verification method possesses different legal evidentiary weight. Please verify requirements with your recipient institution in advance.

Type of Certification Authorized Issuing Body Core Evidentiary Scope Typical Use Cases
Certificate of Translation Certified Administrative Attorney Exact fidelity of translation & statutory certification credentials Official authorities requiring certified translation (Immigration, Universities, Courts)
Notarized Translation Notary Public / Notary Law Firm Official witnessing of the translator's signed declaration Recipients explicitly requesting "Notarization"
Hague Apostille Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Verification of official seals and signatures on public documents Submissions to Hague Apostille Convention contracting states
Consular Legalization MOFA & Embassy Consular Section Final diplomatic validation for cross-border documents Submissions to non-Hague member countries
