Visa, Immigration & Nationality

Tailored legal solutions for Korean Nationality Recovery, F-5 Permanent Residency, F-6 Marriage, and E-7 Professional Employment visas.

🇰🇷

Korean Nationality &
Dual Citizenship

Comprehensive legal assistance for overseas Koreans aiming to restore their Korean nationality, retain dual citizenship, or renounce citizenship according to the complex Nationality Act.

Key Support Items

  • ✔ Korean Nationality Recovery (국적회복) for seniors over 65
  • ✔ Vow Not to Exercise Foreign Nationality (외국국적불행사서약)
  • ✔ Nationality Renunciation & Loss Declarations (국적상실 신고)
E-7

Professional Work Visas
& Talent Hiring

Expert guidance for foreign professionals hired by Korean companies (IT engineers, marketers, executives). We draft comprehensive 'Statement of Employment Reasons' to overcome strict corporate hiring quotas.

Key Support Items

  • ✔ E-7 Specific Occupation Visa Issuance & Extension
  • ✔ Status Change from D-2 (Student) / D-10 (Job Seeker) to E-7
  • ✔ Corporate Eligibility Assessment & Employment Proof
F-5

Permanent Residency &
Marriage Visas

We meticulously evaluate GNI income proofs, KIIP Korean language program completion, and criminal background checks to secure your long-term legal status in Korea.

Key Support Items

  • ✔ F-5 Permanent Residency (General, Points-based, Investor)
  • ✔ F-6 Marriage Migrant Visa (Genuine relationship & income proof)
  • ✔ F-2-7 Points-based Resident Visa Evaluation & Filing

Our Process

STEP 01

Eligibility Assessment

Analyzing income, points, and specific visa requirements.

Translation, Apostille, and drafting justification letters.

Review & Approval

Handling supplementary requests and securing final visa issuance.
