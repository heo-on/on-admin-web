Authorized Legal Representation in Korea

Under the Licensed Administrative Agent Act of Korea, only nationally certified Administrative Attorneys (행정사) are legally authorized to prepare and submit documents to government agencies on behalf of clients. Avoid unauthorized agents to ensure your legal protection.

Recent Case Studies

Examples of our successful solutions for global clients.

Business Setup & FDI

Successful Establishment of Foreign-Invested Corporation & D-8 Visa Issuance

We guided a US-based IT firm through the complex process of FDI reporting, corporate bank account setup, and company registration in Seoul. Following the successful incorporation, we swiftly secured the D-8 Corporate Investment Visa for the CEO.

Visa, Immigration & Nationality

E-7 Special Occupation Visa Approval for a Foreign Engineer

Successfully obtained an E-7 Visa for a foreign mechanical engineer hired by a Korean manufacturing company. We provided a highly convincing employment necessity letter overriding initial concerns from the immigration office.

Corporate Permits & Licenses

Registration for Foreign Patient Attraction Agency

Obtained the highly regulated Foreign Patient Attraction Business Registration from the Ministry of Health and Welfare for a medical tourism startup. We handled the business plan preparation and insurance subscription guidelines.

Translation & Apostille

Apostille Legalization for Corporate Articles of Incorporation

A Korean corporation required their Articles of Incorporation to be submitted to an overseas bank. We provided a certified translation by our licensed Administrative Attorney, and swiftly obtained the Ministry of Justice Apostille certification.
