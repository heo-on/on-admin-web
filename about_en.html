Welcome to Administrative Attorneys Office ON:ON. I am Heo On, Managing Attorney of the firm.

Success in global business begins with meticulous document preparation and full compliance with Korean administrative laws. As an officially registered agency with the Ministry of Justice, we handle foreign direct investment (FDI), corporate visas, nationality recovery, medical permits, KC certification, certified translation, and embassy legalization in a fast, seamless one-stop process.

Drawing on my extensive background as a former military officer (Head of Personnel / Company Commander), I apply rigorous discipline and precise legal interpretation to protect our global clients' interests and deliver optimal results.
