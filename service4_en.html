Nationally certified translation and embassy legalization services. Guaranteeing 100% legal validity for your critical documents worldwide.

Direct Issuance by Certified Public Translator

Under Korean law, a Certificate of Translation can only be legally issued by a nationally certified Administrative Attorney (공인번역행정사). We directly translate and certify your documents, ensuring they are accepted by government bodies, courts, and embassies without notarization delays.

Authentication Types Comparison

Understanding which authentication your document requires.

Type Purpose & Meaning Required When...
1. Certificate of Translation Issued by a nationally certified Administrative Attorney. It legally guarantees the translation is accurate and identical to the original. Submission to Korean government agencies (Immigration, City Halls).
2. Notary Public A lawyer/notary public confirms the signature or the fact that the translation was made. Requested by specific private institutions or foreign courts.
3. Apostille An international certification under the Hague Convention. Bypasses the complex embassy legalization. Submitting documents between Apostille member countries.
4. Embassy Legalization Consular authentication by the embassy of the destination country. When the destination country is NOT an Apostille member (e.g., Vietnam, China).
