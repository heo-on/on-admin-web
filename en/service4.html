📌 Legal Notice on Certificate of Translation Responsibility

A Certificate of Translation is a statutory procedure certifying the consistency and accuracy between the original and translated text:

  • • This document certifies that the translation was faithfully executed and matches the original provided by the applicant.
  • • The translator/administrative attorney is not legally responsible for substantive authenticity or forgery of the original document itself.

Comparison: Translation Certificate vs Notarization vs Apostille

Type Authority Core Certification Applicable Scenario
Certificate of Translation Foreign-Language Administrative Attorney Text consistency & translator's statutory qualification When receiving agency accepts official certified translation
Translation Notarization MOJ Designated Notary Office / Law Firm Notary verification of translator's oath & seal When receiving agency explicitly mandates "Notarization"
Apostille Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Official seal & signature verification of public documents When submitting to Apostille Convention member states
Consular Legalization MOFA & Foreign Embassy Consular Office Final official verification for non-Apostille countries When submitting to non-Apostille member states
